Beyond the time math

Time-recovery is the floor, not the ceiling. A real business case includes three more value levers — each meaningfully larger than time savings, though harder to quantify upfront. Every figure below links to its source.

01

Quality compound

"AI Leaders" achieve 2.3× faster adoption and 67% higher AI ROI than laggards (BCG, 2025). The execution gap is the binding constraint — only 1% of companies are AI-mature despite widespread adoption (McKinsey, 2026).

02

Risk mitigation

The EU AI Act carries penalties up to €35M or 7% of worldwide revenue. And 95% of generative AI pilots never reach production (MIT NANDA, 2025) — structured governance is what closes that gap.

03

Talent positioning

94% of employees stay longer when their company invests in their development (LinkedIn). AI delivers its largest gains to less-experienced staff — a 34% productivity lift for novices (NBER, 2025) — making capability-building a retention and leveling tool.

Methodology & Sources

Calculations assume 2,000 working hours per year per researcher and 48 working weeks. The conservative time-savings default (2.2 hrs/week) is drawn from Federal Reserve research (St. Louis Fed, 2025), which found generative AI users save an average of 5.4% of work hours — roughly 2.2 hours per week. The moderate tier reflects an internal Instacart benchmark (33% operational velocity gain, 2023–2025). The high-adoption tier is supported by NBER (2025) findings of up to 34% productivity improvement for less-experienced workers.

UXR-specific adoption data: Maze Future of User Research Report 2026 — 69% of researchers now use AI in projects (+19% YoY). Capability and ROI-multiplier framing: BCG and McKinsey State of AI.

Currency conversions use approximate fixed rates for illustration only; final engagement quotes are provided in your preferred currency. Year 1 ROI does not include quality compound, risk mitigation, or talent positioning value — these are upside, not floor. Engagement recommendations are based on team size; final scoping depends on tooling environment, regulatory context (EU AI Act, GDPR), and team baseline (calibrated via the free Team Baseline Assessment).
